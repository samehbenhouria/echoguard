🛡️ EchoGuard

A calm second pair of ears for phone calls. EchoGuard listens for the classic moves of a scam call — urgency, secrecy, unusual payment, codes — and tells you, in plain words, pause and verify before money or information leaves your hands.

Agree on this word with your family in person. EchoGuard checks it only in your browser — it is never sent or stored.

Privacy by design: analysis runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is recorded, sent or stored. EchoGuard never asks you to act on a call — it asks you to stop, hang up, and verify through an official channel. Live listening uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome: Web Speech API).

    Responsible AI & limits — what EchoGuard does NOT do

    • It records nothing. Transcripts stay in this page, in your browser. No audio, text or score leaves your device from EchoGuard's own code.
    • It never acts for you. It never calls, pays, blocks or reports anyone. You — a human — always decide, ideally after hanging up and verifying through an official channel.
    • It can be wrong. It is a transparent pattern system, not a mind reader: a clever scam may score low, and an unusual but legitimate call may score high. A low score is never a guarantee a call is safe.
    • It explains itself. Every point of the score names the pattern and the exact words that triggered it, so you can judge the warning instead of obeying a black box.
    • If money or codes already left your hands, contact your bank and the police directly — do not wait for any app.
